# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers looking for remote-friendly tech companies, mostly on their phones: on a commute, on the sofa in the evening, between other things. They skim quickly, narrow down by where they live and what they work with, save a handful of companies, and come back later to read more or apply. Job hunting is tiring and often discouraging, so they arrive hopeful but with little patience for noise. Desktop visitors do the same job with more room: comparing companies side by side and reading profiles in full.

## Product Purpose

Remote Jobs is a fast, friendly directory of remote-friendly tech companies, built on the community-maintained remoteintech/remote-jobs data and enriched with live open roles from public job boards. It answers three questions in seconds: which companies hire remotely where I live, which of them use my stack, and which are hiring right now. Success is a visitor finding a few relevant companies, saving them, and clicking through to a careers page or a specific role.

## Brand Personality

Bright, optimistic, encouraging. The voice is warm and plain-spoken: it talks like a helpful friend who has done the research, not a recruiter or a corporation. It should make the search feel hopeful and manageable. Optimism comes from colour, clarity and kind copy, never from gimmicks.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered job boards (Indeed, LinkedIn Jobs): dense walls of listings, banners, badges everywhere, everything shouting.
- Generic SaaS templates: gradient-blob heroes, identical card grids, stock "startup" look.
- Childish or gamified UI: mascots, confetti, bouncy animations, points and streaks.
- Cold enterprise software: grey, form-heavy, intimidating.

## Design Principles

1. **Answer first.** The list is the product. Search and filters sit right on top of results; nothing stands between a visitor and the companies.
2. **Thumb-first.** Every primary action is reachable one-handed on a phone; desktop is the same app with more room, not a different one.
3. **Colour means something.** Vibrancy comes from meaningful colour (region, hiring, selection), not decoration.
4. **Trust the data, show the source.** Make it clear where information comes from and how fresh it is (upstream profile, live roles fetched N hours ago).
5. **Kind under failure.** Empty results, offline, missing roles and dead links get helpful, specific next steps, never a dead end.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text contrast at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1, in both light and dark themes. Full keyboard and screen-reader support with visible focus. Touch targets at least 44px. Colour is never the only signal (region and status chips are always labelled). Every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
